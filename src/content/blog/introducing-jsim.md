<video src="/videos/jsim-teaser.mov" controls playsinline></video>

Introducing **JSim** a physics simulator for FRC! JSim was built primarily by [@Ruthie-FRC](https://github.com/Ruthie-FRC) from [FRC 5892, the Energy HEROs](https://www.team5892.org/) in collaboration with YASS. This is intended to be a full replacement of MapleSim while integrating much needed features, such as game-piece simulaion, and field collisions with as much accuracy as the programmer allows. JSim makes it easy to provide realistic simulations to any robot and will continue to be maintained for years to come!



We'll have more to share as it gets closer to ready. Follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/yassrobotics) or keep an eye on this blog if you want to be the first to know.
