## What partner teams do
YASS Partner Teams are FRC teams that work closely with the YASS development team to help improve and expand our open-source software. Partner Teams get early access to new features through alpha and beta testing, provide valuable feedback, host workshops, create tutorial videos, improve documentation, and promote YASS throughout the FIRST community.

You can read what the teams have to say, and what each one has contributed, on the [Teams page](/teams).
