Worlds is coming up fast. YALL now supports the 2026.1 firmware, so update before you get there if you're running it.

If your robot is running any YASS library this season, find us at Worlds and we'll hook you up with a sticker. We'll have a few giveaways at our booth, including badge ribbons, and we're hosting another meetup. We probably won't have new libraries ready to demo live just yet, there's still more coming, but we're happy to talk through what we're working on and take feedback, questions, or ideas in person. We're also planning a conference. More on that once there's actually something to announce.

See you there.
